Farm calendar

Lavender takes a few seasons to settle in. Here's what's happening at the farm between now and opening day, and what each season will look like once the gates open in 2030.

The road to opening day

Dates are plans, and farms run on weather. We're building in phases, starting before we even own the land. Sign up for updates and you'll hear as each one happens.

  1. Now

    Help shape the farm Open

    Short surveys for visitors, photographers, chefs and local shops. Your answers decide what we grow and offer.

  2. Phase 0Spring 2027

    The greenhouse and the starts

    We buy our greenhouse and set it up at a friend's farm down the canyon, then bring in 800 small Grosso, Phenomenal, Spanish and French lavender starts to settle in. That way they're ready to plant the moment we have the land.

  3. Phase 12027

    Clearing the land and planting the lavender

    Once the land is ours, the hillside is cleared and prepared right away, and the 800 acclimated starts go in, in rows that follow the slope.

  4. Phase 22028

    The garden, bees and a photography venue

    A deer-fenced organic garden, the greenhouse relocated home from down the canyon, an apiary tucked in the shade beside them, and a glasshouse photography venue beside the lavender. The first farm honey comes in.

  5. 2028

    Private tours and photo sessions By appointment

    Small-group farm tours and portrait sessions in the glasshouse venue and the young lavender rows, booked ahead while the farm grows. Photographers can rent the venue for their own clients.

  6. Phase 32028–29

    The farm building and dinner deck

    A farm shop, workshop space and kitchen for lavender goods, plus a deck for farm-to-table dinners, built while the lavender matures.

  7. 2030

    Open to the public

    U-pick, photo sessions, workshops, farm-to-table dinners and lavender goods made on the farm.

  8. June 2030

    First June Bloom Day Planned

    A free day to wander the rows in full bloom, with lavender lemonade, iced lavender matcha, lavender honey ice cream and u-pick bouquets.
