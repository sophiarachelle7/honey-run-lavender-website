Honey Run Lavender Say hello

A lavender farm is growing in Butte Creek Canyon.

Honey Run Lavender is an organic, permaculture farm near the Honey Run Covered Bridge in Chico. The first plants go in next year. The gate opens in 2030.

Chico's first lavender farm

There's no commercial lavender farm in Chico. The nearest are an hour and a half up the road. We'd like to change that.

I'm Sophia Somers. I fell for homesteading at 22, working on a permaculture farm in Costa Rica, and I've grown food in Hawai'i since. I studied Environmental Science at Chico State, and my day job is in conservation for the Butte Creek watershed, the same creek that runs past this farm.

Honey Run Lavender is the place I've wanted to build for a long time. A small homestead that cares for the canyon's land, with lavender at its center and bees, a garden and good company around it. A place to slow down, take a photograph, and feel like you belong.

How the farm grows

Lavender needs about three years to reach a full harvest. Here's the road from license to open gate.

  1. 2026

    Licensed and planning

    Honey Run Lavender is registered in Chico, and the farm plan and funding are coming together.

  2. 2027

    800 plants in the ground

    Grosso, Phenomenal, Spanish and French lavender start in a greenhouse in spring and are planted out in September.

  3. 2028–29

    Building the homestead

    The apiary, organic garden and gathering spaces take shape while the lavender fills out.

  4. 2030

    Open to visitors

    U-pick, photo sessions, small events, workshops and lavender goods made on the farm.
